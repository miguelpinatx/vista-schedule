Stores

Payroll goals

Payroll budget = each day's sales goal × the store's payroll %. Hour budget = payroll budget ÷ blended hourly rate. Store managers enter their sales goals on the Full schedule tab. Leave a store blank to turn the budget off there.

PINs & store access

With PINs on, everyone must enter a PIN. The admin PIN opens everything: all stores, Dashboard, Staffing guide and Settings. A store PIN opens only that store's schedule and staff. Type a new PIN only where you want to set or change one.

Weekly hour goals

Set a weekly hours goal for each store and, if you want, each department. The dashboard shows how far over or under each one is. Leave blank for no goal.

Hours & rules

Flags in the totals column: full-time under 30 hrs, part-time over 29.99 hrs (ACA), over 40 hrs (needs approval), and more days than the max.
