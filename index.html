Basic mode

For getting started. Stores see just 3 tabs: Employees (roster, department, target hours, days they can't work), Schedule (drag & drop) and Summary (hours by day, department, employee and week, with who's over or under). You see Home and the same Summary to approve. Everything else is hidden, not deleted. Turn this off to bring all the other features back.
Stores, departments, shift buttons and rules are managed by corporate.

Features on / off

Basic mode is on, so these switches are paused. They apply again when you turn Basic mode off.
Start simple and turn on more as your managers get comfortable. Turning something off only hides it; nothing is deleted. Simple setup keeps the schedule, store cards with hours, the days · hours row, and the Summary tab with By department and By shift.

Full-time / part-time rules

When it's on: FT/PT tags show next to names, part-timers are flagged over 29.99 hrs, full-timers under 30, and Fill automatically keeps part-timers at 29.99 or less. When it's off: no FT/PT tags or flags; everyone is checked only for overtime (over 40), personal hour limits and max days. Each employee's FT/PT setting is kept, so you can turn this back on anytime.

Staffing guide on / off

When it's on, schedules are checked against the staffing guide: short / over staffed per department, open spots, and warnings before submitting. Turn it off for a store to hide those checks. Fill automatically still uses the guide's shifts, and you can keep editing the guide.

Payroll goals

Payroll budget = each day's sales goal × the store's payroll %. Hour budget = payroll budget ÷ blended hourly rate. Store managers enter their sales goals on the Full schedule tab. Leave a store blank to turn the budget off there.

PINs & store access

With PINs on, everyone must enter a PIN. The admin PIN opens everything: all stores, Dashboard, Staffing guide and Settings. A store PIN opens only that store's schedule and staff. Type a new PIN only where you want to set or change one.

Weekly hour goals

Set a weekly hours goal for each store and, if you want, each department. The dashboard shows how far over or under each one is. Leave blank for no goal.

Hours & rules

Flags in the totals column: full-time under 30 hrs, part-time over 29.99 hrs (ACA), over 40 hrs (needs approval), and more days than the max.
